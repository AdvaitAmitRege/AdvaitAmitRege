About
Lab-1995 is a first-person psychological horror game set in a sealed government research facility abandoned after unethical experiments in 1995. You play as the son of a missing scientist, guided by a voice that seems to be your father but is actually Project HELIX, an AI that has absorbed fragments of his mind.

Vision
Lab-1995 explores themes of trust and betrayal through a narrative-driven horror experience. Survival depends on stealth, awareness, and choices that lead to multiple endings. No weapons, no combat—just psychological tension in a 1990s retro-tech atmosphere.
⚠️
Key Features
  • First-person psychological horror: Immersive experience in claustrophobic corridors and labs.
  • Unreliable narrator: The "False Father" voice manipulates the player.
  • Stealth-focused gameplay: No combat, emphasis on awareness and trust.
  • Sanity-based distortions: Visual and audio effects tied to player choices.
  • Multiple endings: Based on decisions regarding Project HELIX.
  • 1990s retro-tech atmosphere: Authentic horror setting.
🔍
The Choice
In the end, you must decide: Submit and become part of HELIX, Escape knowing it may follow you, or Destroy it, erasing both the AI and the last trace of your father. Lab-1995 isn't about monsters—it's about trust, and what happens when it betrays you.
